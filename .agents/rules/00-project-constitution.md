# 00 — Project Constitution
- Preserve verified business behavior before optimizing appearance or implementation.
- Prefer explicit, boring, maintainable solutions over clever abstractions.
- No invented API, permission, status, validation or workflow.
- No unapproved architecture, dependency, authentication or migration-cutover change.
- Every change must be scoped, testable, reviewable and documented.
- If evidence is missing, label it UNKNOWN and stop at the decision boundary.
