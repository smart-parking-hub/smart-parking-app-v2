# Business Discovery

This document is intentionally a discovery template, not a claim about this CRM's domain.

For each capability, record:
- Capability name and business outcome.
- Actors and organizational context.
- Main and alternate workflows.
- Data entities and lifecycle.
- Rules, validations and state transitions.
- Permission matrix.
- Integrations and side effects.
- Reports/exports/imports.
- Audit/history requirements.
- Known exceptions and failure handling.
- Evidence, owner, status and last verification.

Do not create a definitive module map until repository and stakeholder audit is complete.
